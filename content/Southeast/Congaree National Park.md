## South Carolina
### This park is definitely dog friendly! Pets are allowed on *ALL* trails and boardwalks, including:

* Boardwalk Loop Trail
* Weston Lake Loop Trail
#### Be sure to check out the B.A.R.K. Ranger program while you're there! 

![](https://www.nps.gov/common/uploads/structured_data/3C862C60-1DD8-B71B-0BB65F7B652BA840.jpg)
The image above shows the Congaree River during Autumn.

**According to the National Park Service, 

> "You have the opportunity to experience the natural wonders of Congaree National Park on foot or on water. With over 25 miles of hiking trails and 2.4 miles of boardwalk, there are many ways to explore the Congaree Wilderness. The boardwalk loop trail provides access to Weston Lake and other trails that wind through the Congaree floodplain. Ranger and volunteer guided programs are led on Saturdays year-round."

Congaree is often compared to Everglades National Park in Florida for its cypress trees and swampy environment.
#### The closest National Park to Congaree is [[Great Smoky Mountains National Park|Great Smoky Mountains National Park]] across the border in North Carolina and Tennessee.
